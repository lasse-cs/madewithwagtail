At wunderweiss we created a new portal that was based on the corporate identity and focussed on the Arabella's core product “radio”. The streaming offer matches the core interests of the listeners. The portal’s design and function are aligned to it and offer fast and problem-free access to audio content and further, programme-related content.

A central platform provides an individual presence for the radio stations Vienna, Lower Austria, and Upper Austria.
