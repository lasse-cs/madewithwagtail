For kronehit, Austria’s private radio station with the largest audience, wunderweiss developed a completely new web site. The focus is, as to be expected from a station “with the most music”, on music. Users have access to a wide selection of music. The striking player offers “seamless” integration of different offers such as moderated and unmoderated channels, podcasts, and music TV. Naturally, the design reflects the modernity of the station.
