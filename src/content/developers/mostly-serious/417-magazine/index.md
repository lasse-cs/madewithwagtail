417 is a life enrichment company stationed in Springfield, Missouri with a suite of publications that includes [417 Magazine](http://www.417mag.com), [Biz 417](http://www.biz417.com), and [417 Home Mag](http://www.417homemag.com). Its new digital presence for those three entities gives it fresh, modern methods to deliver the latest for members of the community interested in being on the forefront of design and fashion trends, business news, local happenings, and much more.
