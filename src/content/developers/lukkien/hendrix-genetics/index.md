Hendrix Genetics is passionate about animal breeding. Hendrix Genetics has breeding programs in turkeys, layers, guinea fowl, swine, salmon and trout.

Hendrix Genetics looks for innovative, sustainable solutions, together with the entire animal protein value chain. At the start of the chain, they have an influence on the outcome. For example, their responsible use of antibiotics program benefits producers and consumers. For Hendrix Genetics, better breeding today, means a brighter life tomorrow.

Lukkien developed a multisite and multilingual website for Hendrix Genetics and their brands. All brands have their own brand identity which have been reflected in the websites, which are easily maintained in one Wagtail CMS.
