The Leprosy Mission works across Africa and Asia to support people affected by leprosy, and they needed a website that they could sustain and manage long term within the realities of charity funding. This project mattered because their legacy platform was becoming difficult to maintain, and they needed a more sustainable and flexible solution.

We worked with their team to redesign and rebuild their website from the ground up, including content management tools, donation journeys and an in-memory giving platform. The focus was on creating a site that could grow with them over time, while remaining manageable for their internal team.

We’re really proud of this project because Wagtail gave The Leprosy Mission full ownership of their platform, allowing them to move to an open-source, sustainable website that their team can manage and develop for years to come.
