Uneven Ground is an interactive report created with the International Land Coalition to highlight the growing issue of land inequality around the world. The project mattered because the research is complex and important, and we wanted to help present it in a way that more people could understand, explore and engage with.

We designed and built an immersive, mobile-friendly digital report that combines storytelling, data and animation to guide users through the findings. The experience is designed to feel more like a story than a traditional report, helping bring the research to life.

We love using Wagtail for interactive storytelling projects like this because it allows us to build flexible, content-driven experiences where content editors can manage and evolve the story over time.
