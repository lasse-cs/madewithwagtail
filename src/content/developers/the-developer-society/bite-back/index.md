Bite Back is a youth-led food justice movement campaigning for a fairer food system, and this was a brilliant project to be part of. The website was designed as a vibrant, mobile-first platform that puts young people’s voices and actions at the centre of the organisation’s work.

The site supports campaigns and youth engagement, including tools that allow supporters to contact MPs, sign petitions, and take part in campaign actions. A big focus for the project was making sure the platform worked brilliantly on mobile and gave the Bite Back team the flexibility to respond quickly as campaigns evolve.

We love using Wagtail for projects like this because it gives teams the freedom to create and manage content themselves, while still supporting complex campaign functionality behind the scenes. It’s a fantastic fit for organisations like Bite Back that need their website to keep up with fast-moving campaigning work.
