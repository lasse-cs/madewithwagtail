Legacy Futures brings together several specialist brands offering research, consultancy and insight across the legacy giving sector. They needed a digital platform that could unite these brands under one roof while still allowing each to retain its own identity and audience. The project mattered because their work is complex and knowledge-driven, and their website needed to support both public content and secure client access in one coherent experience. We designed and built a unified platform that combines a public website with a secure client portal. The site brings together multiple brands, shared resources, news and insights, while clients can log in to access personalised dashboards, documents and research.
