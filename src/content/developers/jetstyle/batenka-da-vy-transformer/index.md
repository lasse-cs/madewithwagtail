"My friend, you are a transformer" is not a media outlet. We are young, professional journalists, artists, illustrators, writers, designers, photographers, managers, pharmacists, corporals, doctors, programmers, archaeologists, and people of many other professions who find our calling in the honest and sincere exploration of the world around us. We call ourselves a samizdat: that means we do everything ourselves, without anyone's investment. We call ourselves the "Order": that means we are united not by profit and salary, but by shared ideals and goals.
