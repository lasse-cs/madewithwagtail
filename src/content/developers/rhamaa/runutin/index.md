Runutin is an Indonesia-based e-learning platform designed to help IT students learn technology in a clear, structured, and beginner-friendly way. It provides step-by-step learning paths, breaking down complex topics like programming, AI, and computer vision into smaller, easy-to-understand lessons. Built for students in Indonesia, Runutin focuses on practical knowledge, helping learners not only understand concepts but also apply them in real-world projects.
