Anita brings over thirty years of experience in the hairdressing industry. With her husband recently retired from the Royal Navy, she has taken the exciting step of opening her own salon for the first time. This website was built to grow alongside her business. It currently supports bookings at her main salon and two remote locations through an integration with Square CRM. Designed with flexibility in mind, the site is ready to adapt as Anita expands to new locations and builds her team. The site is built with Wagtail and designed to be clean, approachable, and easy to navigate. It includes an introduction to Anita’s story, clear service listings, and prominent calls-to-action for online booking.
