The Gewerbemuseum, historically associated with applied arts and design in Switzerland, is a cultural institution dedicated to exploring the relationship between craftsmanship, industry, and visual culture. Through exhibitions, archives, and educational programs, it highlights the evolution of design practices—from traditional techniques to contemporary digital processes—while fostering dialogue between designers, artists, and the public.
