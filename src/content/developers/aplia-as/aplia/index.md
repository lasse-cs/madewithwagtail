We off course use Wagtail CMS on our own websites. Aplia.no is a simple and easy site with information of our company and employees, showcase of some of our costumers, a blog section and job listings of available jobs at our company.

We keep our site clean and easy to navigate, and as one of the first in our industry, 100% cookie-less. Focus on GDPR is getting bigger and bigger, and we want to stay one step ahead and have no first-party or third-party cookies at our sites. To still get important and valuable data from visitors without the use of cookies, we use Matomo Analytics with the features it provides to be GDPR-compliant.
