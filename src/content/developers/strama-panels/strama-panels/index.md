Strama Panels is a Polish manufacturer of modern interior wall panels and aluminium façade systems, serving primarily B2B customers across European markets. The company’s website is built on Wagtail CMS and integrated with the Odoo ERP system, creating a connected platform for content management, sales, customer service and business operations.
