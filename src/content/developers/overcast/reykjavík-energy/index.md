Reykjavík Energy (Orkuveitan) is an Icelandic energy and utility company that provides electricity, geothermal hot water through district heating, and cold water for consumption. Their vision is to enable a sustainable future. On Orkuveitan’s Wagtail website, users can see all information about Reykjavík Energy and its daughter companies, along with environmental, financial, and safety information, and browse through their published content.
