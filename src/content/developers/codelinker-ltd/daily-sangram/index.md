The Daily Sangram, also known as Dainik Sangram, is a Bengali daily newspaper and published from Dhaka, Bangladesh, it is the mouthpiece of Bangladesh Jamaat-e-Islami. The name of the editor is Azam Mir Shahidul Ahsan.
