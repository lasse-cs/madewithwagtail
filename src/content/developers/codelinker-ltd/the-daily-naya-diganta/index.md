The Daily Naya Diganta is a Bengali daily newspaper published in Bangladesh since 2004. It is part of Diganta Media Corporation, which was owned by Mir Quasem Ali. The television channel, Diganta TV, is a sister concern of the newspaper. The television was launched in August 2008. Alamgir Mohiuddin is the editor of Naya Diganta.
