The National Gallery of Australia in Canberra houses the permanent national collection of 155,000 works of art, including the world’s largest collection of Aboriginal and Torres Strait Islander art. We launched this website in December 2021 and integrates Tessitura ticketing and an online collection into a unified visitor experience.
