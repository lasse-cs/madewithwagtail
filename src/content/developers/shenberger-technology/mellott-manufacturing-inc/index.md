Mellott Manufacturing Co., Inc. produces rugged and reliable log and lumber handling equipment. Our products are manufactured in Mercersburg, Pennsylvania USA.

Mellott Manufacturing Co., Inc. has the capability to design and/or fabricate a wide variety of special conveyors and machines for the sawmill, pallet and woodworking industry.
