The University of Duhok (UOD) stands as a pillar of academic excellence, research innovation, and community impact in the Kurdistan Region of Iraq. Established in 1992 with just two colleges, UOD has rapidly evolved into a leading public university, now comprising 19 colleges, 100 academic programs, nearly 24,000 students, and a dedicated team of over 4,000 academic and administrative staff, (UOD FACTS & FIGURES).
