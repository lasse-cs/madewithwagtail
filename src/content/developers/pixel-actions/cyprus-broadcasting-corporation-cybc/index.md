The new website of the Cyprus Broadcasting Corporation is the result of a large digital transformation of the National Broadcasting organisation in Cyprus, building from the ground up their entire digital presence and changing the way they reach their audience.

rik.cy is a comprehensive web platform that integrates all digital mediums of CyBC. Video on demand, Audio on demand, TV and Radio Live Streams, News, Sports and their Digital Archives.
