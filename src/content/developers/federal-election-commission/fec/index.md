The Federal Election Commission (FEC) releases information to the public about money that’s raised and spent in federal elections — elections for US President, Senate, and House of Representatives. FEC.gov is the authoritative source for that data, making campaign finance information more accessible and understandable to all users: how much a candidate raised and spent, their debts, and who contributed to their campaign. The site runs on [fec-cms](https://github.com/fecgov/fec-cms), the open source content management system for FEC.gov, built with Wagtail on the Django framework.
