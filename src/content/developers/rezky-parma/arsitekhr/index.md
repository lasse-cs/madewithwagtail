ArsitekHR is a human resource consultant business site with the addition of course programs, including workshops, webinars, and masterclasses.

Fully built with Wagtail CMS, some of the Wagtail built-in features are used heavily here. The custom form builder flows into a local payment gateway, while the need for a flexible content structure for the marketing team and editorial is easily achieved with StreamField. Last but not least, because Wagtail is just Django, I could find good libraries that work seamlessly with the project.
