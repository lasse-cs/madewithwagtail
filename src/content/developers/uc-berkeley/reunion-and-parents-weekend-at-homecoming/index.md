Reunion and Parents Weekend at Homecoming is UC Berkeley’s largest annual gathering for thousands of alums, parents, and families to come together for three days of fun. On the website, people can search and filter events based on a number of categories. We use Wagtail for the site and HTMX for the event filtering on the home page.
