We built a Wagtail based content manageable website for Letchworth Garden City Heritage Foundation that allows their team to manage their residential and commercial property portfolios, publish news and publications and provide detailed information to residents on planning and the other services they offer. The site features document and image libraries allowing assets to be reused across the site, and makes full use of Wagtails’ publishing workflow.
