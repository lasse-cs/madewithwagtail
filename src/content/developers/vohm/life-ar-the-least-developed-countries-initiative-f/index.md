The Least Developed Countries (LDC) Initiative for Effective Adaptation and Resilience (LIFE-AR) aims to enhance climate resilience by promoting a shift in how climate responses are prioritised, financed, coordinated and evaluated.

We built LIFE-AR as a multilingual Wagtail in English and French to support their mission to enhance climate resilience among some of the world's least developed countries. Translation to French is automated with DEEP-L which is integrated into CMS with a workflow that allows moderators to review and edit before publishing. Post launch, we are providing ongoing creative and technical support, security updates, and hosting on environmentally considerate servers.
