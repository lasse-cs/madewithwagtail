The Edge Foundation seeks to inspire education to give all young people the knowledge, skills and behaviours to flourish. By using Wagtail as their content platform they are able to deliver their message by effectively supporting their campaigns and events. Multirole team editing allows them and bespoke page content elements such as animated counters allow them to build, review and publish engaging content. Post launch, we are providing ongoing creative and technical support, security updates, and hosting on environmentally considerate servers.
