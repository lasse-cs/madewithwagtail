Agenda advocates for the rights of women and girls in the UK and around the world so that all women and girls can thrive. We designed, and built a modern website for them using Wagtail. This allows their team to create and manage engaging content so they can tell their stories in a meaningful way, making the most of their publications and campaigns while bringing together their members and amplifying the voices of the overlooked women and girls they represent. Post launch, we provide ongoing creative and technical support, security updates, and hosting on environmentally considerate servers.
