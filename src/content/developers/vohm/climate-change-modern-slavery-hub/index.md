A joint initiative between Anti-Slavery International, OKUP and the International Institute for Environment & Development, the Climate Change & Modern Slavery Hub aggregates evidence on the intersection of climate change, migration and vulnerability to modern slavery.

Using Wagtail, this site custom built for Anti-Slavery International allows the project partners to geolocate their data points and publications by adding them to an interactive, filterable map, helping evidence the intersections of Climate Change, Migration and Modern Slavery in a visual and easy to follow way. The site uses a custom styled map which displays records and publications which are added and managed as content pages in Wagtail.
