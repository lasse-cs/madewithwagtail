We recently helped EDocuments with a stalled web build and branding project.
After engaging with another agency and not being quite happy with the result, Edocs approached us for help. Working within the building/construction sector, they wanted to bring more of a tech-startup feel to their brand without alienating or discouraging some of their more traditional client base. We certainly didn't rubbish the previous work, we repurposed some of the better ideas and redeveloped the designs with improved execution. We redesigned and re-platformed the website into Wagtail CMS giving a far slicker, more tailored, and less confusing content management experience.
The board and management teams were delighted with the final result.
