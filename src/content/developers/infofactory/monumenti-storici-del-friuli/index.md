“Monumenti Storici del Friuli" is a website that collects the contents for tourists visiting the area of the 75 booklets that make up the series of the same name. They present detailed information on monuments, churches and palaces located in the territory of
provinces of Udine and Pordenone, and represent a resource to be exploited to allow tourists to discover new places and sites to visit related to the culture of Friuli.
Information is offered detailed related to individual places of cultural interest, in order to increase dissemination of the contents of the booklets.
