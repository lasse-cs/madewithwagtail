DIUM aspires to operate in the broadest spectrum of humanities and to give his research a double and complementary thrust: in fact he is driven not only by the will of conservation and enhancement of cultural heritage, but also the need to go further. To seek the deepening of new spaces and directions of application for research, and the promotion of that interdisciplinary approach that today characterizes the scientific landscape in a broad sense.
