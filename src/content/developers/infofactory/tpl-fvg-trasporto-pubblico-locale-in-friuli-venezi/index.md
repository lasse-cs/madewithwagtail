TPL FVG is the consortium of Local Public Transport of the Friuli Venezia Giulia region by road and by sea.
Thanks to the experience of the consortium companies, TPL FVG is able to guarantee the quality, flexibility and specificity of the service on the regional territory, with the added value of an integrated approach to public transport .
Innovation, new technologies, maximum ease of access to services and information: the goal of TPL FVG is to constantly improve every aspect of the travel experience for residents and tourists, starting from programming, purchasing the ticket until arrival at destination.
