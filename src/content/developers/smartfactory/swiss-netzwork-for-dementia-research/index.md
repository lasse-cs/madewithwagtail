The Swiss Network for Dementia Research connects researchers, healthcare professionals, and institutions across Switzerland to foster collaboration, knowledge exchange, and innovation in dementia research. Developed for the Synapsis Foundation, the platform promotes scientific networking, clinical translation, and improved patient outcomes through digital collaboration.
