Rape Crisis England & Wales supports victims of sexual violence, offering a range of incredibly important information such as what to do if you've been attacked recently to how to cope with an assault that may have happened in the past. It also has a Helpline. It's available and accessible to victims, friends and family, and practitioners.

RCEW is also a membership organisation, offering information and support to organisations across England and Wales working with victims at a local level.
