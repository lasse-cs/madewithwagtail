Esmée Fairbairn Foundation is one of the UK's largest independent funders, giving more than £50M in grants to organisations making the world a better place.

It also runs a £45M social investment fund.

Esmée's Wagtail site is predominantly to inform funders and funding partners of their work, mission and funding opportunities.
