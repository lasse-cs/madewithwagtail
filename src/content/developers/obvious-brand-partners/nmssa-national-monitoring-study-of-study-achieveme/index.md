NMSSA is a large-scale assessment that monitors trends in student achievement through Aotearoa New Zealand. NMSSA is a collaboration between the University of Otago, the New Zealand Council for Educational Research and the Ministry of Education.

NMSSA approached Obvious Brand Partners for a redesign and rebuild of their outdated website. Empowering the NMSSA team to be able to manage their content themselves, building the new website on Wagtail and making use of Streamfield's module-based page builder was a no brainer.
