In an intensive design process a framework was developed, which allows the employees of the museum to maintain the page with as little effort as possible. The permanent exhibition of the museum founder - Friedensreich Hundertwasser - is an equal part of the numerous current activities of the Kunsthaus. The simple modern design gives ornamental art a pleasantly clear frame.

The Webshop of the Kunsthaus Vienna offers more than 100 unique products, most of which are exclusively made. To ensure a consistent appearance of the website and the webshop, the shop has been redesigned and developed completely new and responsive. Users can now quickly find out about the product range and purchase the desired products via the intuitive and inviting user interface.
