Oxfam GB’s new flagship Wagtail-powered digital home. The new website has delivered a 16% uplift in donations, a huge reduction in ongoing costs (compared to the legacy Sitecore platform) and has enabled the organisation to unify their donations platform, events and blogs microsites on the centralised Wagtail platform, making it easier and quicker to manage content and significantly reducing ongoing maintenance and hosting costs.
