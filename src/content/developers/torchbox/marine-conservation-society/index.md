We worked with the Marine Conservation Society through a drastic digital transformation project, which included:

- Bringing the new brand to life on a modern, future-proof CMS, Wagtail, which has seen an increase in visits by 105% and a reduction in bounce rate by 20%;

- Designing, prototyping and launching the Good Fish Guide, a mobile app used for sourcing sustainably caught fish which now receives an average of 4000 visits per day;

- A major CRM integration with ThankQ.

In the few months since the launch membership has increased by 32% and 1,200+ beach cleaners have logged onto their personal volunteering dashboard.

“We’ve loved working with the Torchbox team and are really pleased with the results. The new site is easy for the team to use and really helps us bring our work to life – inspiring more people to take action and help save our seas.” Tamsin Betti, Director of Engagement and Communications, Marine Conservation Society.
