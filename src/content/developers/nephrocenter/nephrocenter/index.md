The network of dialysis clinics "Nephrocenter" is already a leader in the Ukrainian dialysis market. Only this year, our company opened four modern centers - in Kiev, Lviv, Odessa and Zaporizhzhi. More than 400 patients already use the services of our centers, and our patients receive the full range of services free of charge - Nephrocenter operates under the Medical Guarantees Program.

The Nephrocenter plans to expand the dialysis network and create comfortable conditions for patients throughout Ukraine. Thanks to the program of medical guarantees and cooperation with leading manufacturers of dialysis equipment, Nefrocenter has the opportunity and desire to develop this area in our country - the patient deserves quality, timely and comfortable treatment!
