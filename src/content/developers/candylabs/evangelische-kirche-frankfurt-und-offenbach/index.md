The website www.efo-magazin.de is the digital magazine and website of the Evangelische Kirche Frankfurt & Offenbach all in one.
Candylabs built a clear information architecture with a modern design and a content-first strategy. The goal was to build a platform which leads the users efficiently throughout the page to their searched object.

The technical infrastructure of Django and Wagtail used for the project allowed the development and design alike maximum freedom of design in implementation as well as an easy process for further development.

If you like to learn more about this case go to: https://www.candylabs.de/de/case-studies/evangelische-kirche/
