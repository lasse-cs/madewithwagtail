OvaNet is a global consortium dedicated to advancing ovarian tissue cryopreservation (OTC) and transplantation (OTT) for fertility preservation in girls and women at risk of premature ovarian insufficiency or treatment-related fertility loss. Recently founded by Stine Gry Kristensen and Christiani Andrade Amorim, the consortium aims to promote best practices and coordinate clinical and research efforts, recognising that only through collaboration is it possible to establish robust, consensus-driven directives.
To support this international effort, OvaNet has developed an online platform designed to meet the unique needs of a distributed, interdisciplinary community. The platform will serve not only as an operational backbone for the consortium but also as a catalyst for coordinated research and transparent outcome comparison. Wagtail CMS supports most of the tools being implemented in the platform.
