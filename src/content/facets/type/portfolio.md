These Wagtail sites are portfolios: showcases of creative or professional work, from photographers to design studios. The collection includes Little Truffles Photography, the development studio Evonove, and Octavian Gaming's online portfolio. A portfolio needs strong imagery and fast pages, and Wagtail gives editors drag-and-drop image handling with automatic renditions while developers keep full control of markup and performance. Many of these portfolio sites were built by small studios for themselves or their clients — a good starting point if you are commissioning something similar.
