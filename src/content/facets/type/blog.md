These Wagtail sites are blogs: ongoing editorial publications rather than brochure sites. The collection includes GuitarParty, a guitar tab and lesson site; El Noticiero, the web outlet of a Honduran television news programme; and the media monitor ActiveWatch. Wagtail works well for blogging because editors get a clean writing interface, drafts and scheduled publishing come as standard, and the framework stays fast as archives grow. Many of the blogs here are part of larger Wagtail sites — the CMS handles marketing pages and editorial content side by side.
