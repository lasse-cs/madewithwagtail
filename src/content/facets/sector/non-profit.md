Non-profit organisations make up the largest sector in the Made with Wagtail showcase. Examples include the global health organisation PATH, the Agenda Alliance for Women's and Girls' Rights, the media monitor ActiveWatch, and the Letchworth Garden City Heritage Foundation. Charities and NGOs choose Wagtail because it is open source with no licence fees, meets accessibility standards out of the box, and is simple enough for non-technical staff to update themselves.
