Technology companies and products use Wagtail both for their own marketing sites and as the CMS behind developer-facing documentation and blogs. This collection includes LearnDataSci, a data science education platform, and Voxpow, a speech recognition tool for websites. As a Python/Django project itself, Wagtail resonates with technical teams: it is extensible, has a documented API, integrates with modern front-end stacks, and is backed by an active open source community. Many of these sites were built in-house by the companies themselves rather than by agencies.
