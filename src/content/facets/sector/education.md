Schools, universities, and education programmes use Wagtail for everything from prospectus-driven university sites to school enrolment platforms. This collection includes Medborgarskolan, one of Sweden's largest study associations; New Zealand's National Monitoring Study of Student Achievement; and EnrolHQ, an enrolment platform used by schools. Wagtail suits education websites well: its page hierarchy handles large faculty and course structures, granular permissions let many departments publish safely, and content workflows keep editing simple for busy academic staff.
