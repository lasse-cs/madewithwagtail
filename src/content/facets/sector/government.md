Government and public sector organisations use Wagtail at every level, from municipal councils to national agencies. Examples in this collection include the US Federal Election Commission and the Consumer Financial Protection Bureau, Hampshire Constabulary and Thames Valley Police in the UK, and the Pennsylvania boroughs of Mercersburg and Greencastle. Public bodies choose Wagtail because it is open source, meets accessibility standards, and offers fine-grained editing permissions — keeping citizen-facing sites transparent and vendor-independent.
